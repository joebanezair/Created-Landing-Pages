Questions clients will ask before they hire.

Answering these clearly reduces uncertainty and builds trust.

How do you screen candidates?

Explain your actual process: application review, structured interview, communication assessment, reference checks where appropriate, and role-specific testing. Only claim checks you really perform.

Where are your professionals based?

Our initial talent focus is the Philippines. Role location and working hours are confirmed before interviews.

How quickly can I receive candidates?

Set a realistic service target after you have tested your sourcing workflow. Avoid guaranteeing a turnaround until you know you can consistently meet it.

What happens if the hire is not the right fit?

Your replacement policy should state the support window, eligibility, exclusions, and whether additional recruitment fees apply.

Who employs and pays the worker?

This depends on the service model. Your contract should clearly identify whether the client hires directly, the worker is an independent contractor, or a compliant employer/payroll partner is used.
